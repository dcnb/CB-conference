## About the Conference

The National Interdisciplinary Lookout Conference brings together scholars, artists, and creative practitioners from across disciplines to explore new directions in research and creative expression. Our 2026 conference theme focuses on innovative approaches to interdisciplinary collaboration and emerging methodologies.

This annual gathering provides a platform for presenting cutting-edge research, engaging in meaningful dialogue, and building lasting connections across academic and creative fields. Whether you're sharing your latest research, participating in workshops, or networking with colleagues, the Lookout Conference offers opportunities for professional growth and intellectual exchange.

### Who Should Attend?

- Researchers exploring interdisciplinary methods
- Graduate students presenting their work
- Artists and creative practitioners
- Academic professionals seeking collaboration opportunities
- Anyone interested in the intersections between fields
