## Become a Sponsor

Support scholarly exchange and cultural heritage preservation! Sponsorship opportunities are available at multiple levels.

### Benefits of Sponsorship

**All Sponsors Receive:**
- Recognition on conference website and materials
- Logo display at conference venue
- Acknowledgment in opening and closing remarks
- Post-conference impact report

**Platinum ($5,000+)**
- Everything above, plus:
- Exhibit table at conference
- 4 complimentary registrations
- Featured logo placement
- Opportunity to address attendees

**Gold ($2,500-$4,999)**
- Everything in Silver, plus:
- 2 complimentary registrations
- Premium logo placement

**Silver ($1,000-$2,499)**
- Everything in Bronze, plus:
- 1 complimentary registration
- Logo on conference program

**Bronze ($500-$999)**
- Logo on website
- Recognition in printed materials
- Social media recognition

### In-Kind Sponsorship

We also welcome in-kind contributions:
- Venue space
- A/V equipment
- Catering and refreshments
- Printing services
- Software licenses
