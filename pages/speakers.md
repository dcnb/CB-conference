## About Our Speakers

The National Interdisciplinary Lookout Conference brings together scholars, practitioners, and community members from across disciplines to explore the history, preservation, and future of fire lookout structures.

Our speakers include:

- **Keynote Speakers**: Leading scholars and practitioners offering major addresses
- **Presenters**: Researchers sharing original work in panels and paper sessions
- **Workshop Leaders**: Experts facilitating hands-on learning experiences
- **Session Chairs**: Distinguished scholars moderating panel discussions
- **Organizers**: The team bringing this conference together
