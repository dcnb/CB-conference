## Getting to Moscow

### By Air

**Pullman-Moscow Regional Airport (PUW)**
- 8 miles from campus
- Daily flights to/from Seattle and Boise
- Rental cars and shuttle service available

**Spokane International Airport (GEG)**
- 90 miles north of Moscow
- Major hub with connections nationwide
- Shuttle service and rental cars available

### By Car

Moscow is easily accessible by car:
- **From Spokane:** 90 miles south on US-195 (1.5 hours)
- **From Boise:** 290 miles north on ID-55 and US-95 (5 hours)
- **From Seattle:** 300 miles east via I-90 and US-195 (5.5 hours)

**Parking:** Visitor parking is available on campus. Conference registration includes a parking permit.

### By Bus

- **Wheatland Express:** Daily service between Pullman-Moscow and Spokane Airport
- **Northwestern Trailways:** Regional bus connections
